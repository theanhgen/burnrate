# Privacy Policy for burnrate

**Effective Date:** April 7, 2026

## 1. Introduction
This Privacy Policy explains how **burnrate** ("we," "us," or "the App") handles your data. We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring a transparent user experience.

## 2. Data Collection
**burnrate does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data or usage information to our servers.**
- We do not have any backend servers.
- We do not use third-party analytics or tracking tools (e.g., Google Analytics, Firebase).
- We do not collect crash reports that contain personally identifiable information.

## 3. Third-Party AI Provider Data
The App functions by fetching quota and usage data directly from the AI providers you configure (e.g., Anthropic, OpenAI, Google, GitHub).
- **Credentials**: Any API keys, session cookies, or tokens you provide are stored securely in your macOS Keychain or local encrypted storage. They are never sent to us.
- **Direct Communication**: All communication with AI providers happens directly between your Mac and the provider's API. No data is proxied through our systems.
- **Provider Policies**: Data fetched from these providers is subject to their respective privacy policies. We encourage you to review the policies of any provider you use with burnrate.

## 4. Local Data Storage
All configuration settings and cached quota data are stored locally on your device. You can clear this data at any time by resetting the App or deleting its local storage.

## 5. Changes to This Policy
We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Effective Date" at the top.

## 6. Children's Privacy
This App is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect any personal information from children.

## 7. Contact Us
If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please open an issue on the burnrate project: [https://github.com/theanhgen/burnrate/issues](https://github.com/theanhgen/burnrate/issues).
